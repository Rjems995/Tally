Delete your Tally account

Use Tally in your browser or the mobile app to delete your account and its saved data.

  1. Log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile and select Delete account.
  3. Confirm your password and select Delete account and data.

This removes your account, receipt details, images, items, saved category corrections, sessions, and reset tokens from the active database. It cannot be undone.

Files you already exported remain wherever you saved or shared them. The service operator must describe any backup retention in its published privacy policy.

If you forgot your password, use Forgot password? on the login screen first.
